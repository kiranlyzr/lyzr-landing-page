Why it matters

Everything in front of the decision is done by hand: the research, the tagging, the memo. The decision, the only part that needs your team, gets what time is left.

The problem it was built for

Observed across corp dev and ventures teams
Coverage is capped by headcount Four or five people, researching each company by hand and writing the memo. Hundreds of thousands of companies in the market, and you cover what four people can read.
The list is out of date before it is used Funding, leadership, pivots. A company scored in March is a different company by June, and the refresh costs what the build cost.
Nobody can say why you passed without redoing the work The committee asks why these three and not those. The reasoning is in a deck, an inbox and one analyst's memory, so someone rebuilds it.
