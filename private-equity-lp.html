FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Why not just use Claude™ or ChatGPT™? We already pay for that.

A chat gives you an answer. A firm needs a record.

Ask the same question twice and you get two answers, and nobody owns either. A chat cannot tell you that Tuesday's QoE contradicts an assumption your committee approved in March: it holds neither the assumption, the model, nor the decision. And the mathematics does not belong in a language model.

We already pay for three tools that each do part of this. What happens to those?

Your data subscriptions are not the overlap. A PitchBook™ or Capital IQ™ key plugs in and runs on your subscription, and Spotlight sits above the systems your information already lives in rather than replacing them.

The overlap is the layer above. The tool that screens, the tool that reads the data room, the tool that drafts the memo. Three of those is not three times the coverage, it is three records of the same deal that cannot pass a finding between them.

How do we size the business case?

Against hours, not against a deal. Fee earners put nearly 40 percent of their own work in the automatable column, and an average of 25 hours a week on the table.

Take a third of that across your investment team and ask what an hour is worth here. That is the number, it is yours rather than ours, and it is the same whether the year is busy or quiet.

Is this running for a private equity firm today?

The Lyzr platform is, in production, inside customers' own environments. Accenture, WTW, Hitachi, NTT Data and AWS, each of them built around how that organisation works rather than switched on out of a box. In private capital, FTV Capital is a customer with a published case study.

Your investment workflows do not exist until we build them with you, and that is the offer rather than a gap. We do not ship one investment process and ask every firm to adopt it. On a private deployment they are built around how your firm actually works, scoped per engagement, which is why the firms we are working with now are the ones shaping them.
