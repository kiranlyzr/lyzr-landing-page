The problem

Everything in front of the decision is done by hand.

01

Coverage is capped by headcount

Four or five people, researching each company by hand, tagging it and writing the memo. There are hundreds of thousands of companies in the market. You cover what four people can read.

02

The list is out of date before it is used

Funding, leadership, pivots. A company scored in March is a different company by June, and keeping the list current costs what building it cost.

03

Nobody can say why you passed without redoing the work

The committee asks why these three and not those. The reasoning sits in a deck, an inbox and one analyst's memory, so someone goes back and rebuilds it.

How it works.

05
The question every corporate development team asks first

The agents do the grunt work, so your analysts make faster and better decisions.

The agents
Thesis
  • Turn a priority or a mandate into a structured thesis, with criteria and weightings
  • Generate the search query from that thesis
Market map
  • Read every company in the universe, not the slice a team can get through
  • Pull and normalise every connected source you already pay for
  • Deduplicate, and flag a company you have already looked at, with who looked and when
Evaluation
  • Score every company against every criterion, at your weightings
  • Attach the evidence to each individual score, with the link it came from
  • Write the evaluation narrative, including founder and cap table detail
  • Answer why any company scored the way it did
Rework
  • Run a completed scan again against changed priorities, without redoing the work
  • Re-tag and re-sort an existing list when the criteria move
Outputs
  • Draft the investment memo
  • Draft the CEO briefing
  • Produce the committee ready one page output
After the decision
  • Keep every score, override and pass with the reasoning behind it
  • Monitor the portfolio and surface movement
  • Assemble the recurring status view across mandates and business units
Your team keeps
  • The mandate. What you are looking for, why, and how much for
  • The criteria. What counts, what it weighs, and when that changes
  • Any override. Any score, on any company, with the reason recorded
  • Who is worth a call. Selection stays with the team
  • The founder call. Every part of it
  • Judgment. Reading the room, reading the founder, what is in no dataset
  • The pass. Deciding not to proceed, and recording why
  • Approval. Signing off the memo before it goes up
  • After the term sheet. Spotlight ends at the memo
06
One company, more than one mandate

Evaluate once. The evidence decides whether you buy, invest or partner.

Acquire

Corporate development
  • The capability is strategically critical
  • Control is required
  • Synergies are material
  • Waiting creates strategic risk

Invest

Ventures
  • The market is still emerging
  • Access and information rights are valuable
  • Further validation is required
  • Acquisition optionality is preserved

Partner

Commercial or alliance
  • Speed to market matters
  • Commercial integration is sufficient
  • Ownership is unnecessary
  • Capabilities are complementary

Monitor

Not yet
  • Strategic relevance is real but early
  • The thesis has not been proven
  • Timing is wrong rather than the company
  • Revisit when the market moves
And because the evaluation is kept with its reasoning, the second team to look at that company starts from it rather than starting again. Ventures and corporate development stop arriving at the same company from nothing.
07
Working in it

How your team actually works in it.

Ask, or click. Both work

Ask for a market map, a score or a memo the way you would ask a colleague, or click through it the way you would any other tool. Neither route is the poor relation.

Everyone in the same room

Comments sit on the theme, the company and the memo section they belong to, with a named approver and a trail. No version of anything gets emailed around.

Make it yours

Every step, criterion and weighting in the workflows we ship is yours to change. Your process does not have to bend to ours.

Build what we do not ship

Base workflows solve sixty to seventy percent. We build the rest with you, on the same sources and the same controls.

No AI expertise required

Nobody has to learn prompting to get an output. The workflow already knows what it is doing.

It remembers what you decided

Every thesis, score, override and pass is kept with the reasoning behind it. The next time that company comes up, it opens knowing what you already concluded.
